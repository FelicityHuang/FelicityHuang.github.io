The problem

Sustainability is becoming essential. But for small brands, the data is everywhere.

Product information often sits across supplier emails, material sheets, certificates, spreadsheets and founder knowledge. MiniBrand turns that scattered information into a clear, credible product story.

01

Scattered files

Supplier documents, images and spreadsheets are difficult to manage across every product.

02

Unclear claims

Brands need to communicate sustainability without vague or unsupported language.

03

Limited capacity

Small teams rarely have dedicated compliance, data or sustainability departments.

04

Hidden value

Materials, craft and sourcing choices often remain invisible to customers.

How it works

From scattered product data to a living product passport.

MiniBrand keeps the workflow simple: collect information, structure it with AI support, then publish a passport your customers can scan and trust.

1

Collect

Upload supplier documents, product images, material details, certificates and care information into one structured workspace.

2

Understand

AI extracts key information, identifies missing evidence and helps organise sustainability claims into clear product data.

3

Publish

Create a beautiful Digital Product Passport with a QR code for your website, swing tag, packaging or retail display.
